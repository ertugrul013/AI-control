# AIControl demo film: three storylines

The one thing: an SME can see in one afternoon which AI it uses, how risky each use is, and what to do about it.
Spine: one IT manager answers one question from the board, using the real AIControl screens (localhost:3000, demo data).
Every screen is captured from the running app; nothing is drawn as a mockup.

## A. The board's question (recommended)

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day / object-to-object chain.
- **What you see:** an email from the board asks "Which AI tools do we use, and are we ready for the AI Act?". The IT manager answers it in AIControl: register, classify, policy, training. The answer is the dashboard, and that same dashboard goes back to the board as the report.
- **First 2 s:** a full-frame email in the real mail-client style, subject "AI Act: where do we stand?", with the cursor on the last line: "Can you tell us by Friday?"
- **Beats:** hook (email) → tension (a spreadsheet of tool names with "?" in every risk cell) → reveal "AIControl" → proof: quick-add ChatGPT, classify CV screening tool (the live preview flips to High on the recruitment answer), "Why high" with Annex III(4) and the actions it creates, publish the policy, an employee ticks off a to-do → payoff: the risk strip fills segment by segment and the summary sentence lands, "386 days" → claim → end card with `docker compose up`.
- **Look:** white paper ground, Public Sans, the app's own tokens; the tier colours are the only colour, and the risk strip is the one material that recurs.
- **Sound:** calm minimal pulse around 100 bpm that opens at the reveal; clicks on real clicks, small pops as the strip segments land, one riser into the payoff.
- **Length:** about 40 s.

## B. Inside the strip

- **Device / form:** the result first / an endless zoom.
- **What you see:** the film opens on the finished risk strip and dives into its segments one by one. Each segment opens into the screen behind it: the hatched one becomes Grammarly being classified, the orange one becomes "Why high", the red one becomes "Stop use immediately". It ends by pulling back out to the whole dashboard.
- **First 2 s:** the strip fills the frame, nine segments and one hatched gap; the camera starts pushing into the gap.
- **Beats:** hook (strip) → dive into the gap: classify, the gap takes a colour → dive into High: rules and actions → dive into Prohibited: stop use → pull back: the dashboard and its summary sentence → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the same app tokens; the camera never cuts, and every world is a segment of the strip.
- **Sound:** a low drone that rises one step at each dive, with a whoosh on each zoom and one impact on the pull-back.
- **Length:** about 30 s.

## C. 386 days (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a number lands / cut on the beat.
- **What you see:** giant kinetic type counts down to the date high-risk obligations apply, then fires the questions an SME cannot answer ("Which AI do you use?", "How risky?", "Who is trained?") in hard cuts on the beat. Each question is answered by one real AIControl screen for half a beat.
- **First 2 s:** "386" fills the frame in Public Sans 600, with the subtitle "days until 2 December 2027".
- **Beats:** hook (number) → three questions, each with its screen → tool names stack fast and are sorted into tier colours → payoff: the dashboard snaps together → claim → end card.
- **Look:** colour fields in the tier palette with huge type, cutting every half beat.
- **Sound:** a driving 120 bpm track; cuts land on the beat and there are no other effects.
- **Length:** about 25 s.
